# Banking-Customer-Risk-Loan-Analytics-Dashboard

# 🏦 Banking Customer Risk & Loan Analytics Dashboard

An end-to-end Business Intelligence project that transforms raw banking data into actionable insights using **Python**, **SQL**, and **Power BI**. The dashboard helps financial institutions analyze customer behavior, evaluate marketing campaign performance, assess loan exposure, and identify high-value customer segments to support strategic decision-making.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Banks collect vast amounts of customer and marketing campaign data, but deriving meaningful insights from this data can be challenging. This project analyzes customer demographics, financial behavior, and marketing campaign performance to provide interactive dashboards that help stakeholders make informed business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze customer demographics and financial profiles.
- Evaluate marketing campaign effectiveness.
- Segment customers based on Customer Lifetime Value (CLV).
- Identify customer risk and loan distribution.
- Monitor subscription trends across different customer groups.
- Support data-driven business decisions through interactive visualizations.
